**zezinho's Train Timer 1.0.0**

Shows when the armored train arrives and when it leaves (Reserve and Lighthouse).

- On the train's row of the extraction list, the timer counts down to the arrival, then to the departure, and the exit name says "(arrives in)", "(leaves in)" or "(has left)".
- Optional on-screen line with the same times, off by default.
- English and Portuguese.
- Client only.

**Install:** extract the zip into your game folder. With Fika, every player who wants the times needs the mod.
